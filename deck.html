The problem

2 / 14
  • A refinancing produces lender term sheets in different formats; a misread covenant or fee can reach the client
  • About 60% of 2025 UK lending was refinancing
  • Debt funds' market share of UK commercial property lending has risen from 12% to 28% since they arrived about a decade ago
  • 13% of tested loans had interest cover below 1x
  • Founder: lender canvasses of 70+ lenders and 15 to 25 term sheets on a single deal

Projections are not results.
Known failure modes: labels without colons or with dotted leaders shift values by a line; two LTV figures; split-tranche margins. Fixing these is the technical co-founder's first job. The maturity view is not a growth channel: the CCOD licence bars direct marketing to registered proprietors and charges are a weak maturity signal.
