Product today: a prototype

4 / 13

Compare

Lender term sheets side by side; every value linked to its source text; draft memo for adviser review.

Size

Deterministic loan sizing: LTV, ICR and debt-yield limits, binding constraint, refinancing gap, rate stress.

Maturity view

Experimental lender-relationship view, shown on synthetic data only.

The reader is a prototype that flags uncertain fields. On new layouts our own red-team test found confident errors in about 5 of 88 field checks, so a person checks every value.

Projections are not results.
Known failure modes: labels without colons or with dotted leaders shift values by a line; two LTV figures; split-tranche margins. Fixing these is the technical co-founder's first job. The maturity view is not a growth channel: the CCOD licence bars direct marketing to registered proprietors and charges are a weak maturity signal.
